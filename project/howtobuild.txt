To build Stage 1 (the Vocal Formant Multiple-Feedback Bandpass Filter) in LTspice using your exact BOM values ($R_1 = 22\,\text{k}\Omega$, $R_2 = 10\,\text{k}\Omega$, $R_3 = 100\,\text{k}\Omega$, $C_1 = C_2 = 10\,\text{nF}$, and $C_{\text{in}} = 10\,\mu\text{F}$), think of the circuit as three separate sections that meet at a central junction node before going into the op-amp:   Power Section: Two DC voltage sources (+9V and -9V) wired strictly to the op-amp's power pins (V+ and V-).   Input Series Chain: Your audio source goes through Cin (10u) in series with R1 (22k), landing at a middle junction node (let's call it Node_M).Branching & Feedback Around Node_M and the Op-Amp: From Node_M, the path splits into three parallel branches—one to ground (R2), and two (C1 and C2) going toward the op-amp's inverting input (In-) and output (OUT), with R3 (100k) in parallel with C2 in the feedback loop.Step-by-Step LTspice Wiring Order1.Build the ±9V Split Power Supply:Keep power completely separate from audio.Place two voltage sources (voltage) and two Ground symbols (GND / key G).Positive Rail: Wire the - terminal of V_pos to GND, and wire its + terminal to the V+ pin of your TL072 symbol (set V_pos to 9).Negative Rail: Wire the + terminal of V_neg to GND, and wire its - terminal to the V- pin of your TL072 symbol (set V_neg to 9, or wire + to V- and set value to -9).Verification: Neither V_pos nor V_neg touches any resistor, capacitor, or signal input pin—only GND, V+, and V-.2.Ground the Non-Inverting Input (In+):Zero-volt reference for the op-amp.Connect the In+ pin of the TL072 directly to GND (or through your second 10k ground-bias resistor to GND—both work identically in LTspice since the TL072 has near-zero JFET input bias current).Verification: Pin In+ has a clear path to GND and does not connect to In- or OUT.3.Wire the Input Series Path:Input source → Cin → R1 → Node_M.Place your input signal source (V_in, set to AC 1 for frequency sweeps), Cin (10u), and R1 (22k) in a horizontal line from left to right:Wire the - terminal of V_in to GND.Wire the + terminal of V_in in series into the left side of Cin (10u).Wire the right side of Cin in series into the left side of R1 (22k).Label the wire coming out of the right side of R1 as Node_M (press F4 to add a net label) so you can easily see where everything branches.Verification: Tracing from V_in (+) through Cin and R1 to Node_M is one single unbranched line.4.Add the Shunt Resistor (R2) and Split Capacitors (C1, C2):Three branches meeting at Node_M.From Node_M, branch out to three components:Branch 1 (Shunt to Ground): Connect the top of R2 (10k) to Node_M, and wire the bottom of R2 to GND.Branch 2 (To Inverting Input): Connect the left side of C1 (10n) to Node_M, and wire the right side of C1 directly to the In- pin of the TL072.Branch 3 (To Op-Amp Output): Connect the left side of C2 (10n) to Node_M, and wire the right side of C2 directly to the OUT pin of the TL072.Verification: When you hover over Node_M in LTspice, four component pins highlight together: R1 (right), R2 (top), C1 (left), and C2 (left).5.Close the Feedback Loop and Add an Output Load:R3 in parallel with C2 between In- and OUT.Complete the active filter loop around the op-amp:Connect the left side of R3 (100k) to the In- pin (where C1 already connects).Connect the right side of R3 to the OUT pin (where C2 already connects)—this puts R3 in parallel with the C1–C2 tee network across the op-amp.Connect a load resistor R_load (10k) from OUT to GND so the output has a clean reference load for simulation.Verification: In- connects only to C1 and R3; OUT connects only to C2, R3, and R_load.Quick Topology Summary (Series vs. Parallel)In Series: V_in $\rightarrow$ Cin (10u) $\rightarrow$ R1 (22k) $\rightarrow$ Node_M.   Shunt to Ground from Node_M: R2 (10k) to GND.   Series Path from Node_M to In-: C1 (10n) $\rightarrow$ In-.   Feedback Path to OUT: C2 (10n) runs from Node_M to OUT, while R3 (100k) runs from In- to OUT.
