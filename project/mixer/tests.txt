=================
--- .ac sweep ---
=================

set either V_voice or V_violin to AC 1 while the other is AC 0
enter .ac dec 100 20 20k
Run and click mix_out. You should see a flat 0 dB line from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, with 180° phase (inverted).
Click spk. It should be the same, except the gain rolls off below about 1.6 Hz from C_out and R_load.
Swap the AC 1 source to confirm the violin channel behaves identically.

==========================
--- .tran two-tone sum --- 
==========================

Set V_vocal to SINE(0 0.5 880) and V_violin to SINE(0 0.5 440).
enter .tran 0 50m 0 10u uic
Plot V(mix_out). You should see the 440 Hz and 880 Hz tones added together and flipped, 
with peaks of up to about 1 V.
